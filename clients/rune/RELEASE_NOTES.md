**Phux Rune 0.1.0-alpha.6**: updates and machines.

- **Updates**, the GPUI desktop's flow: checks at launch and every six hours,
  a corner card, a real download percentage, minisign and checksum
  verification, install on quit or **Restart now** (back on your session). It
  also updates the phux CLI and hands the live server off without closing
  sessions. Settings has the switches. This release is the last manual
  download: alpha.5 and earlier can't update themselves.
- **Machines**: switch the window to any registered remote from under the
  sidebar's title, or add one with `me@host` or from your tailnet. If your
  sessions live on another computer, this is how you reach them.
- **⌘W closes right away.** **Confirm before closing** in Settings brings the
  question back, for tabs too.
- Rune moved to the latest `dev`.

Signed, notarized and stapled. Alpha: one window on one machine, no Kitty
images yet. See
[clients/rune/README.md](https://github.com/no-phux/phux/blob/main/clients/rune/README.md).
