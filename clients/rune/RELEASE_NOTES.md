**Phux Rune 0.1.58**

- Swipe the terminal sideways to page between sessions: it follows your thumb,
  names the session it is heading to, ticks a haptic where letting go commits,
  and rubber-bands at either end.
- Pull from the left edge to slide Sessions in as frosted glass over the
  terminal it dims; let go early and it slides back out.
- Return sits under the right thumb, leading the scrolling strip at the largest
  text sizes. The ⇧ latch remains available for local selection in mouse-tracking
  programs and one-shot key chords.
- Reconnecting cancels an old finger's session or drawer gesture. A session
  switch that cannot land brings its page back even while the terminal is idle.
- Sessions group active panes and bring attention forward. Hold a row for its
  actions, using the system menu on iOS; delayed actions stay bound to that
  original row and computer. Done and New Tab join the native glass controls.
- Fill the phone with the terminal edge to edge (#3003).
- Attach photos, camera shots and files from the iOS key bar (#3007, #3014).
- A lock screen card, Dynamic Island and agents widget on iOS that stay
  current while the app is closed (#3008, #3010).
- Keep every paired computer on iOS and switch between them (#3006).
- Phone-preview colours get reliable touch targets (#3005).
