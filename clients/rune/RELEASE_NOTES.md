**Phux Rune 0.1.0-alpha.10**: every theme, and yours.

- **Twenty-six themes.** The GPUI desktop's whole set, dark then light, as
  tiles in Settings; arrow keys still preview them live.
- **Your Ghostty theme.** A Ghostty config on this machine becomes the
  **Ghostty** theme: its `theme` file, `config-file` includes and colour
  lines, read at launch.
- **Your Omarchy theme.** On Omarchy, the **Omarchy** theme follows the
  current Omarchy theme, colours and accent, and repaints when you switch.
  It is the default until you pick another theme; picking one sticks.
- **Themes from the palette.** Type a theme's name and press Return;
  **Change Theme** opens the grid.
