**Phux Rune 0.1.0-alpha.20**: creatures that grow with work done, a smooth reef and interactive surface panes.

- **Creatures grow with work done.** In the Reef and Galaxy, each agent's
  creature grows with the tasks it finishes and the time it spends working:
  fry, reef fish, elder, then an exotic form (lionfish, manta ray, seahorse,
  cuttlefish or octopus by agent) and finally a legendary one with a trail of
  light. Galaxy nodes gain rings, moons and a corona the same way. A gold ring
  marks each level-up. Needs a server running the `com.phux.agent-stats`
  plugin; without it every agent stays a reef fish.
- **Waiting agents spread out.** Agents that need you no longer pile up at the
  surface; on a crowded reef they stagger into two rows.

- **The reef runs smoothly.** Reef frames drop from about 40 ms to about
  7 ms with 30 agents, so the view stays fluid with a busy fleet. The look
  is unchanged.
- **Remote reefs show their counts.** A machine you watch now shows its
  agent count on its reef buoy, not just its fish.
- **Click and type into surface panes.** When a surface's program accepts
  input, clicking its pane clicks the frame under the pointer, the wheel
  scrolls it, and while it is focused typing and Enter, Tab, Backspace,
  Escape and the arrows go to it. Cmd and Ctrl shortcuts stay Rune's.
- **Answer questions asked before Rune opened.** An agent question raised
  before this window connected now shows its text in the inbox and pane
  card, so you can answer it there.

Includes alpha.19's spatial phone navigation and focused Settings.
