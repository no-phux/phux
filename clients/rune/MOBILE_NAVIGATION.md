# Phone navigation

How you get around the phone form: the top bar, the Sessions page, the
session switcher, the swipes between and away from them, and where each
page returns. The rest of the phone is in [Phux Rune on a phone](./MOBILE.md).

The terminal is the root. Sessions is its drawer from the left edge; every
other page is a sheet that rises from below. Each page leaves the way it
came, by its own control or a swipe, and returns to the page that opened
it.

| Piece | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Top bar: back opens the searchable Sessions list, carrying the needs-you dot (and naming the count) while a pane other than the one on screen asks; tap the title to open the session switcher (Connections when there is no pane). Its subtitle names the session, the pane's state and, on iOS, the computer; the terminal screen spends no row on a computer pill. The switcher leads with its title, then All sessions and Close at the trailing edge, as every page keeps its close. Its computer pill opens Connections: on iOS UIKit's sheet over the switcher, which waits under it. Swipe its terminal preview or use the arrows to browse; tap the preview or session name to switch; swipe it down to close. Previewing reads one pane's screen without attaching, resizing, or typing into it. Close returns to the original terminal. One touch-target row: back, a plain title, and ⋯ (terminal actions, What's new's unread dot). Split tabs retain their pane map and pane picker | `src/ui/phone/bars.rs`, `src/ui/phone/navigation/switcher.rs`, `src/desk/phone/switcher.rs` |
| Sessions: a dedicated scrollable browser with the current session and pane marked, pane/agent state and attention counts, empty sessions, search across session/pane/agent/folder names, and a named or one-tap new session. Search raises the keyboard when tapped; a 44-point Clear search control restores all results and keeps the editor ready for another query. Done (or a swipe left) closes it without clearing the filter or changing the terminal's keyboard choice: the next visit opens with the same search, where it was in the list; picking a row is done with the search. Opened with no search, it opens at its top, Connections and Needs you whole; only when the pane on screen would be wholly below the fold does it open on that pane's session instead, its card whole at the top (at the large text sizes, where search scrolls with the list, always at its top). Connections opens native connection setup on iOS, over Sessions; the desktop-hosted phone also browses other machines' CLI-backed session summaries | `src/ui/phone/navigation.rs`, `src/desk/phone/navigation.rs` |
| Sessions is an inset grouped list. Needs you leads with asking or unread-finished panes, each naming its session, unless the card below lists it. Tap the Needs you heading, or its Open Inbox disclosure, to answer in the Inbox, which comes back to Sessions on closing; assistive technology still reads the band's name as a heading. Tap anywhere on a session header to collapse or expand its panes. Empty sessions and search-result headers open the session directly. Quiet sessions share a disclosure that previews their names, and search shows matching panes regardless of folding. A session header's ⋯, on the column the panes' checks share, or a hold on it, opens Open, New Pane, Fold, Rename and Close Session; hold a pane for Open, New Pane Here and Close Pane. iOS presents the system menu from the held row. A close that would ask shows its question over the target pane. Session headers show pane count, current status, working count, configured colour and attention count. Pane titles sit above agent/state or folder detail, with a badged glyph, and a check on the current pane. At ordinary text sizes search stays fixed, Connections scrolls with the list, and New session has a dedicated footer below it, with native Liquid Glass on iOS. At large text sizes all of it scrolls, and the action is opaque. Focusing search hides Connections for keyboard room and editing the filter returns to the first result. The left-edge gesture brings up Sessions over a frosted terminal (opaque under Increased Contrast and Reduce Transparency). The navigator shows loading, empty-inventory and no-match messages as appropriate. Sessions and Terminal actions have no accessory key bar; search Return finishes editing. Terminal actions retain grouped cards, end the Inbox/Settings group with What's new, put Close Terminal last in the failure tone, and keep Back to terminal under the thumb below the list (native Liquid Glass on iOS). Inbox, Settings, What's new and the desktop-hosted Machines sheet opened from a page come back to that page on closing, however they close; one that left for another pane (choosing any pane, including the Inbox's Return on an empty draft, cancels the return, even to the pane already on screen or one still loading), or gave way to another surface, does not. | `src/ui/phone/navigation.rs`, `src/ui/phone/navigation/menu.rs`, `src/mobile/host.rs` (`held_menu`, `menu_pick`), `ios/Sources/HeldMenu.swift`, `src/desk/phone/navigation.rs`, `src/desk/phone/runtime/navigation/` |
| Sideways thumb on the terminal: the page follows the finger one to one and pages to the neighbouring tab (a third of the stage, or a flick). Past the session's last or first tab it steps over into the next session in sidebar order, heavier on purpose: the page gives less than the finger travels, commits at half the stage (a flick a tab's length), and plays a firmer detent. The uncovered strip names where a lift goes in plain type, a tab by its name and a session under a Next or Previous session line, lit once lifting commits; the first and last sessions rubber-band. A drag that starts within 32 points of the left edge pulls Sessions in instead: the terminal frosts over in step with the finger while the list drifts in behind it, opening past 30% or on a flick, and fading back off when let go short. A light haptic detent marks each commit point on the native host. Vertical drags still scroll | `src/desk/phone/swipe.rs`, `src/ui/spatial.rs`, `src/ui/phone/navigation.rs` |
| Every page goes the way it came: a swipe left takes Sessions back to its edge, a swipe down lowers Terminal actions, the switcher, the Inbox and the palette, and a swipe in from the left edge takes Settings back a level. The page follows the finger one to one through live values (no rebuild or layout while it moves) and commits as the terminal's swipe does: past 30% of the way, or on a flick, with a detent as it crosses; let go short, it springs home on the drawer spring; under Reduce Motion it lands home, or leaves, at once. A drawer or a level fades as it goes, Sessions ahead of its frost, which clears after it so the list never shows over a crisp terminal; a sheet slides whole, clipped above the key bar. The Inbox carries a sheet's grabber at its top. The native host's touches reach the swipe first, as a system gesture recogniser sees them: a tap or a hold within the slop is still the row's, the first movement on any other axis is Rune's scrolling, a list scrolled from its top keeps a downward drag, and no swipe starts on a search or reply field. Each closes as its own control does | `src/desk/phone/dismiss.rs`, `src/mobile/host.rs` (`touch`), `src/ui/phone.rs` (`swipeable`) |
