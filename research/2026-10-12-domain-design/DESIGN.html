Resource → Session → Workspace

Resources are things. Sessions group them. Workspaces link sessions across machines.

Square = a view of a resource. Same work, different layouts on desktop and phone.

Close square → work keeps running

Kill is a separate, explicit action.

Empty named session → it stays

Quick sessions are ephemeral, by choice, not by accident.

Delete workspace → sessions stay

It owns links, not their execution.

Agent acts → it is attributed

Agents act through grants. A human can always take the keyboard back.

Who owns what?
  • Core: one model for identity, membership, and lifecycle.
  • Owning server: records, execution, access, and who did what.
  • Clients: views, layout, and focus. No alternative session rules.
The constraints that keep this honest
  • One resource → one session → one owner.
  • A session can be linked into several workspaces.
  • Names and IP addresses are not identity.
  • Offline does not mean dead.
  • Saved organization does not mean processes survive reboot.
  • Browsers and other kinds have their own capabilities—not fake terminal fields.
